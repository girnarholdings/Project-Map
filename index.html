Markets & Trading

Five tools that do the overnight reading nobody has time for — 115 news sources, hours of video, a whole equity universe — and hand you one page you can act on before the open. Every one of them is live right now. Open any card and see this morning's output.

Walk in already knowing · three editions a day

BoltNews

Nobody reads 115 sources before the bell, so most people trade on the two they happened to open. This reads all of them overnight and gives you a single page: what moved, why it moved, and what is set up for today — across stocks, bonds, oil, gold and crypto, in plain English.

The best market commentary is buried in hours of video you will never watch. This watches all of it and gives you each creator's actual thesis in a paragraph — so you hear the call while it is still tradeable instead of a week late.

Stop fighting the market's current

BoltFactors

Being right about a company and wrong about the regime still loses money. This tells you which style is actually paying right now — value, momentum, quality or safety — and refreshes five times a day, so you are trading with the tide rather than into it.

The hard part was never the analysis — it is knowing which handful of names deserve it. Seven independent passes rank the entire universe overnight and hand you a short list that is already sorted, each name carrying the reason it made the cut.

Earnings are where the largest moves happen and the most money gets left on the table. This looks ahead at who is about to report, scores which of those setups is actually worth taking, and writes out the case for each one — so you are positioned before the print rather than reacting after it.
